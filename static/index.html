💡 High-Conviction Product Hypotheses

Problem-space hypotheses adhering to SEBI direct-broker compliance and calm brand ethos.

P0 Priority

1. PayDay-Aligned Smart SIP & 1-Tap Skip

Problem: Rigid debit dates collide with variable salary credit dates, triggering punitive ₹295–₹590 bank ECS bounce penalties that destroy investor trust.
Hypothesis: Allow dynamic debit date shifts matching actual salary credits, combined with a zero-penalty 1-tap skip before bank submission.

P0 Priority

2. SIP Volatility Shield (Drawdown Contextualization)

Problem: When an equity fund enters a normal -3% to -8% market correction in Month 4-6, users with an FD mindset panic, misinterpret volatility as a scam, and terminate their SIP.
Hypothesis: Replace alarming red banners with institutional historical recovery bands (e.g. 3-year Nifty 50 positive rolling return probabilities) to anchor investor patience.

P1 Priority

3. Decoupled 1-Tap Pause vs Cancel

Problem: When festive or emergency cash crunches occur, users cannot find a temporary pause option and are forced to delete their SIP or revoke mandates in NetBanking.
Hypothesis: Separate temporary 1-3 month cashflow relief from irreversible mandate cancellation in the SIP management flow.

P1 Priority

4. Statutory SMS & T+2 Settlement Tracker

Problem: First-time investors panic when receiving official regulatory SMS from ICCL/BSE StAR MF or experiencing the standard T+2 business day NAV allotment window.
Hypothesis: Proactively educate users during Month 1 onboarding that ICCL/BSE SMS alerts are SEBI-mandated clearing, and provide a visual pizza-tracker for unit settlement.
